We build solutions with partners — not for them. Relationships and collaboration are at the heart of everything we do.

We begin by listening. Our team conducts deep discovery sessions to understand your organization's specific risk landscape, operational environment, leadership structure, and security maturity level — ensuring every recommendation is grounded in your reality.

Executive Cybersecurity Strategy

Our firm develops tailored cybersecurity strategies that align with your organization's specific needs, risk landscape, and operational goals. Whether building a Security Operations Center (SOC) from the ground up, enhancing governance practices, or strengthening cybersecurity maturity, our approach ensures a comprehensive and resilient solution.

We create roadmaps that account for both strategic risk management and long-term growth, regulatory compliance, and operational resilience.

Crisis Management Solutions

Cybersecurity incidents represent significant crises requiring coordination and structured responses. ODS takes a comprehensive view, working across the phases of mitigation, preparedness, response, and recovery.

Our services include cybersecurity incident response and coordination plans, training programs, tabletop exercises, Incident Command management, legal and regulatory navigation, PR support, and data retention compliance.

By approaching incidents as crises, we ensure organizations are equipped not only to respond but to recover and strengthen their resilience.

Government Affairs & Business Development

Open District Solutions helps companies leverage strategic government affairs to enhance business development opportunities. Our firm identifies key opportunities in the SLTT and SLED sectors, aligning business strategies with government needs and positioning organizations for success in these critical areas.

Workforce Development & Partnerships

Solving workforce development challenges is essential in the cybersecurity space. Our firm partners with cybersecurity ranges, academia, and innovative apprenticeship programs to develop and implement solutions that bridge the talent gap.

These programs connect organizations with emerging talent while creating pathways for future growth and skills development.

Cybersecurity Assessments & Maturity Development

ODS conducts thorough cybersecurity assessments to help organizations identify vulnerabilities and gaps. Our firm provides detailed roadmaps for improving cybersecurity posture, covering architecture reviews, governance, risk and compliance (GRC), and maturity assessments.

A core part of these assessments is understanding and integrating risk management into your cybersecurity strategy.

Cybersecurity Investment & Architecture

Building a strong cybersecurity program is not just about tools or technology — it's about making strategic investments over time. Our firm works with organizations to develop phased investment plans that align security needs with budget constraints, ensuring scalable, cost-effective solutions.

Our expertise in cybersecurity architecture ensures organizations are protected with robust, resilient security measures that evolve and mature over time.

Netta Squires
President · Gov Affairs · Cybersecurity & Resilience

Netta Squires is a cybersecurity strategist and resilience leader with over 20 years of public safety experience. As Maryland's first Director of Local Cybersecurity, she spearheaded Whole of State initiatives and served on the MS-ISAC Executive Committee.

She led Montgomery County's Cybersecurity Program, directed COVID-19 response efforts, and managed multi-agency national capital region operations. She co-chaired Maryland's SLCGP Committee and architected the National Capital Region's Cybersecurity Mutual Aid Operations Plan.

Netta is an Adjunct Professor at UMGC and Frederick Community College. She holds a JD from GWU Law, a Public Leadership Credential from Harvard Kennedy School, an MSL in Cybersecurity Law, and is a Certified Emergency Manager (CEM).

Jayda Bonnick
Cybersecurity Strategy Analyst

Jayda Bonnick is a Cybersecurity Strategy Analyst at ODS, bringing a strong background in public policy and communications. She is pursuing a Master's in Global Security, Conflict, and Cybercrime at New York University, where she co-founded the Global Cyber Students Club and serves as an editor for the Global Affairs Review.

Prior to joining ODS, Jayda honed her media strategy skills in public relations at Nike Communications. She holds a B.A. in International Relations from Boston University and leverages her multidisciplinary expertise to help organizations strengthen their cybersecurity resilience.

Ashley Sterbach-Benson
Cybersecurity Program Manager
