Executive Cybersecurity Strategy
Our firm develops tailored cybersecurity strategies that align with your organization's specific needs, risk landscape, and operational goals. Whether building a Security Operations Center (SOC) from the ground up, enhancing governance practices, or strengthening cybersecurity maturity, our approach ensures a comprehensive and resilient solution. We create roadmaps that account for both strategic risk management and long-term growth, regulatory compliance, and operational resilience.
Crisis Management Solutions
Cybersecurity incidents represent significant crises, requiring coordination and structured responses. Open District Solutions takes a comprehensive view of crisis management, working across the phases of mitigation, preparedness, response, and recovery to provide full support during incidents.

Our services include the development of cybersecurity incident response and coordination plans tailored to specific operational environments. Our firm also designs training programs and facilitates tabletop exercises to ensure teams are prepared for any eventuality.

During incidents, our firm provides hands-on guidance by establishing and managing Incident Command to streamline coordination and decision-making. We assist with navigating legal and regulatory requirements, including reporting obligations and data retention, while ensuring that the response is managed at the appropriate operational tempo. Our team offers Public Relations (PR) support, helping organizations manage external communications and protect their reputation.
Government Affairs & Business Development
Open District Solutions helps companies leverage strategic government affairs to enhance business development opportunities. Our firm identifies key opportunities in the SLTT and SLED sectors, aligning business strategies with government needs and positioning organizations for success in these critical areas.
Workforce Development & Partnerships
Solving workforce development challenges is essential in the cybersecurity space. Our firm partners with cybersecurity ranges, academia, and innovative apprenticeship programs to develop and implement solutions that bridge the talent gap. These programs connect organizations with emerging talent while also creating pathways for future growth and skills development.
Cybersecurity Assessments & Maturity Development
Open District Solutions conducts thorough cybersecurity assessments to help organizations identify vulnerabilities and gaps. Our firm provides detailed roadmaps for improving cybersecurity posture, covering areas such as architecture reviews, governance, risk and compliance (GRC), and maturity assessments. A core part of these assessments is understanding and integrating risk management into your cybersecurity strategy.
Cybersecurity Investment & Architecture
Building a strong cybersecurity program is not just about tools or technology — it's about making strategic investments over time. Our firm works with organizations to develop phased investment plans that align security needs with budget constraints, ensuring scalable, cost-effective solutions. Our expertise in cybersecurity architecture ensures organizations are protected with robust, resilient security measures that evolve and mature over time.
Netta Squires
President · Government Affairs · Cybersecurity & Resilience
Netta Squires is a cybersecurity strategist and resilience leader with over 20 years of public safety experience, 15 of which have been in the public sector. As the Founder and President of Open District Solutions, Netta leads a team dedicated to helping organizations navigate complex security challenges, implement robust resilience strategies, and foster cross-sector collaboration.

Prior to relaunching ODS, Netta was appointed as Maryland's first Director of Local Cybersecurity, spearheading Whole of State initiatives and serving on the MS-ISAC Executive Committee.

She is an Adjunct Professor at UMGC and Frederick Community College, holds a Juris Doctorate from GWU Law, a Public Leadership Credential from Harvard Kennedy School, and an MSL in Cybersecurity Law and Policy. She is a Certified Emergency Manager (CEM) and Certified Cyber Resilience Professional (CCRP).
Jayda Bonnick
Cybersecurity Strategy Analyst
Jayda Bonnick is a Cybersecurity Strategy Analyst at Open District Solutions, bringing a strong background in public policy and communications to her work. She is currently pursuing a Master's in Global Security, Conflict, and Cybercrime at NYU, where she co-founded the Global Cyber Students Club and serves as an editor for the Global Affairs Review.

Prior to joining ODS, Jayda honed her media strategy skills in public relations at Nike Communications. She holds a B.A. in International Relations from Boston University and leverages her multidisciplinary expertise to help organizations strengthen their cybersecurity resilience.
Ashley Sterbach-Benson
Cybersecurity Program Manager
Ashley Sterbach-Benson is a cybersecurity program manager supporting state, local, tribal, and territorial (SLTT) governments through program management, stakeholder engagement, and applied cybersecurity research.

With over three years of experience across national cybersecurity, government, nonprofit, and public–private partnership initiatives, she specializes in aligning SLTT perspectives with federal and national partner priorities. At the Center for Internet Security, Ashley has functioned as a program manager supporting executive governance, strategic initiatives, and organization-wide alignment in service of a diverse SLTT membership.
